About DeskLabPK

Minimalist, ergonomic desk accessories designed for focus, comfort, and zero clutter. we believe your workspace shapes how you think, create, and work. Whether you are coding late into the night, designing your next project, or studying for exams, a cluttered desk shouldn't get in the way of your focus.We started DeskLab with a simple goal: to make clean, ergonomic, and thoughtfully designed desk setups accessible without the inflated price tags. Every item in our catalog is chosen to solve a real everyday workspace problem—from eliminating neck strain with precision-machined laptop stands and reducing desk echo with merino wool felt mats, to hiding tangled wires and lighting up your desk without screen glare.

💡 Planned Business Idea

DeskLabPK solves the problem of cluttered, uncomfortable workspaces by offering an affordable, matching matte-black and Nordic grey ecosystem—combining desk organization, lighting, and peripherals in one store.

🎯 Marketing & Sale Strategy

  • Dual Flash Sales: Promotional pricing on the LED Ambient Light Bars (Rs. 4,999) and 75% Mechanical Keyboard (Rs. 6,999) to drive high-intent traffic.
  • Short-Form Video: 15-sec TikTok & Instagram Reels "Before/After" desk transformations.
  • Cross-Selling: Upselling the Rs. 1,199 Felt Desk Mat and Rs. 1,899 Headphone Stand to maximize the Rs. 300 flat shipping value.

🚚 Logistics & Area of Success

  • Flat-Rate Shipping: Transparent Rs. 300 nationwide delivery across Pakistan (3–5 working days).
