🖥️ DeskLab — Minimalist Workspace & Desk Setup E-Commerce StoreDeskLab is a responsive Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) e-commerce web application tailored for university students, software developers, and remote workers across Pakistan. Built using vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, it combines a modern storefront with an interactive Business Plan & Unit Economics Dashboard.🌐 Live Demo: https://najeebahmed69.github.io/desklab-ecoomerce/✨ Key FeaturesDynamic Product Catalog: 8 curated workspace essentials across 5 categories (Organization, Lighting, Ergonomics, Peripherals, and Accessories) with live search and category filtering.Full-Page Product Detail Views: Clicking any product opens a dedicated view with high-resolution imagery, full descriptions, and technical specifications.Interactive Cart & Local Checkout:Real-time quantity controls and dynamic PKR (Rs.) currency formatting.Flat Rs. 300 nationwide shipping calculation.Pakistani payment options: Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash / Easypaisa, and Bank Transfer.🎓 15% University Student Discount: Buyers can verify their university status at checkout using a .edu.pk email or roll number to automatically deduct 15% off their product subtotal.📊 Integrated Business Plan Dashboard: A dedicated executive view detailing KPIs, marketing strategy, hybrid sourcing & micro-warehousing logistics, unit economics, and Google Keyword Planner search demand.Mobile-First Responsive Design: Optimized navigation bar and grid layouts for both desktop and mobile browsers.📦 Product Catalog & Pricing
