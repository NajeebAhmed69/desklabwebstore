# 🖥️ DeskLab — Minimalist Workspace & Desk Setup E-Commerce Store

**DeskLab** is a responsive Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) e-commerce web application tailored for university students, software developers, and remote workers across Pakistan. Built using vanilla **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**, it combines a modern storefront with an interactive **Business Plan & Unit Economics Dashboard**.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://najeebahmed69.github.io/desklab-ecoomerce/](https://najeebahmed69.github.io/desklab-ecoomerce/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Dynamic Product Catalog:** 8 curated workspace essentials across 5 categories (*Organization, Lighting, Ergonomics, Peripherals,* and *Accessories*) with live search and category filtering.
- **Full-Page Product Detail Views:** Clicking any product opens a dedicated view with high-resolution imagery, full descriptions, and technical specifications.
- **Interactive Cart & Local Checkout:**
  - Real-time quantity controls and dynamic PKR (`Rs.`) currency formatting.
  - Flat **Rs. 300** nationwide shipping calculation.
  - Pakistani payment options: **Cash on Delivery (COD)**, **JazzCash / Easypaisa**, and **Bank Transfer**.
- **🎓 15% University Student Discount:** Buyers can verify their university status at checkout using a `.edu.pk` email or roll number to automatically deduct **15%** off their product subtotal.
- **📊 Integrated Business Plan Dashboard:** A dedicated executive view detailing KPIs, marketing strategy, hybrid sourcing & micro-warehousing logistics, unit economics, and Google Keyword Planner search demand.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Design:** Optimized navigation bar and grid layouts for both desktop and mobile browsers.

## 📈 Business Model Overview

- **Target Audience:** University students, programmers, designers, and remote professionals in Pakistan.
- **Projected Monthly Traffic & Conversion:** 5,000 monthly visitors at a **3.5%** conversion rate (**175 orders/month**).
- **Blended Average Order Value (AOV):** **Rs. 4,250** (factoring in 15% student discounts on ~40% of orders + Rs. 300 shipping).
- **Projected Monthly Gross Revenue:** **Rs. 743,750**
- **Estimated Net Profit Margin:** **~42%** (**~Rs. 311,875/month** after COGS, courier fees, packaging, and digital ad spend).
- **Supply Chain & Logistics:** Hybrid bulk sourcing from Shenzhen (Alibaba/1688) and local wholesale hubs in Karachi & Lahore, stored in a zero-rent home micro-warehouse and fulfilled nationwide via PostEx, Trax, and Leopards Courier.
